Games ive played and my opinions

Part 1: Jet Set Radio (2001)

Jet set radio is a skating game set in a futuristic japan where players control members of a street gang called the GGs. The game is known for its vibrant art style, catchy music, and fast-paced gameplay. Its definitley one of my personal favorites ive played. Someone should totally get me a dreamcast so i dont have to emulate it again T-T

Part 2: Bomb Rush Cyberfunk (2023)

Bomb rush cyberfunk is a skating game set in New amsterdam (YES ITS A REAL PLACE. STOP ASKING ME IF IT IS) where one of the Bomb rush crew members looses his head in an accident with a rival gang, and in attempt to get his head back, you battle other rival graffiti and skate gangs to reclaim New amsterdam and go "All City." I really enjoyed playing it and the gameplay is super fun and trippy, some people say its even the spiritual succesor of Jet set radio.

Part 3: Danganronpa: Trigger Happy Havoc (2011)

A little bit on the different side of things, Danganronpa: Trigger Happy Havoc is a visual novel and adventure game where players take on the role of a high school student who becomes trapped in a school and must solve the murders of their fellow students to survive. The game is known for its engaging story, memorable characters (like nagito komaeda and Junko Enoshima. wink wink), and unique gameplay mechanics. It reminded me alot of Ace attorney when i played it, it took a while to finish it but its a really sick experience.
